MedEx Heart Anatomy 3D — Asset Credits and Scope

Asset: heart-bp3d.bin
Generator: MedEx Procedural Heart v1 (2026)

This asset contains original, procedurally generated teaching geometry created from signed-distance chamber surfaces, curved vessel surfaces, and constructed meshes for valves, coronary branches, papillary muscles, chordae tendineae, trabeculae, and the conduction system. The current binary does not contain the earlier BodyParts3D mesh geometry and does not embed a third-party model.

Important scope note: the geometry is an idealised educational visualization. It is not derived from a patient scan, has not been clinically validated, and must not be used for diagnosis, procedural planning, or clinical decision-making. Verify anatomical details against a trusted anatomy atlas and subject-matter expert review before using it as an authoritative teaching reference.
