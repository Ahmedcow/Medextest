heart-bp3d.bin — 3D heart anatomy data used by MedEx "Heart 3D"

Derived from BodyParts3D (release 3.0):

  licensed by CC Display-Inheritance 2.1 Japan
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/
  Mitsuhashi N, et al. BodyParts3D: 3D structure database for anatomical concepts.
  Nucleic Acids Res. 2009;37:D782-D785. doi:10.1093/nar/gkn613  (data DOI 10.18908/lsdba.nbdc00837-000)

Modifications: meshes re-oriented/scaled, vessels trimmed, heart wall partitioned into chamber regions,
simplified (quadric decimation), ambient-occlusion and epicardial-fat shading baked, flow and conduction
paths added. This derived data is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 2.1 JP licence.


Additional processing in this updated file:
  Vertex normals were recalculated from each mesh's triangle geometry using area-weighted face normals, then blended with the source normals (75% recalculated / 25% source) to smooth lighting while retaining some of the original shading character. Geometry, triangle indices, mesh-part definitions, and the existing ambient-occlusion/epicardial-fat data were preserved. The binary container and original filename are unchanged.
