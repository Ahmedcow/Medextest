Source meshes used (full-resolution BodyParts3D STL files, FMA concept ids):
  wall of heart (FMA7274); aortic, pulmonary and tricuspid/mitral valves (FMA7234, FMA7235, FMA7246);
  papillary muscles (FMA7260, 7261, 7262, 7266, 9352); ascending/arch/descending aorta (FMA3736, 3768, 3784);
  pulmonary trunk/arteries (FMA66326); pulmonary veins (FMA66643); SVC (FMA4720); IVC (FMA10951);
  brachiocephalic, carotid and subclavian branches; coronary arteries and cardiac veins
  (FMA3802, 3818, 3840, 3862, 3895, 4685, 76994, 71669, 71670, 4706, 4707, 4713, 71567, 76751 and branches).

Modifications (v5.2): the heart wall and vessels were voxelised, the chamber cavities were separated
(LV, RV, RA, LA) with a watershed on the hollow interior of the wall, vessel lumens were hollowed and
opened at their cut ends, aortic and pulmonary root conduits were generated where the source wall has open
orifices, regions were labelled (chambers, septa, vessels, branches), and the outer surfaces were remeshed
and Taubin-smoothed; the aortic and pulmonary trees are single continuous surfaces; blood-flow animation paths are computed through the hollow lumen. The aortic valve leaflets, the ligamentum arteriosum and the epicardial-fat /
ambient-occlusion shading were generated for this model. Interatrial and interventricular septa labels are
rule-based approximations.
This is a single-individual, educational model; it is not intended for surgical or diagnostic planning
and has not been reviewed by an anatomist.
