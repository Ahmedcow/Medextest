Modifications (v6 model): meshes re-oriented/scaled, heart wall partitioned into chamber regions and
smoothed; aorta, arch branches, pulmonary arteries/veins, SVC and IVC re-modelled as smooth, tapered,
thick-walled vessels fitted to centrelines extracted from the BodyParts3D meshes (procedurally generated
surfaces, with open cut ends); right and left atrial auricles (appendages) re-modelled as separate
conformal parts; aortic root with sinuses of Valsalva added; simplified (quadric decimation),
ambient-occlusion and epicardial-fat shading baked, flow and conduction paths added.
Coronary arteries and cardiac veins are generated schematic tubes following the groove anatomy.
This is a single-individual, educational model; it is not intended for surgical or diagnostic planning.
